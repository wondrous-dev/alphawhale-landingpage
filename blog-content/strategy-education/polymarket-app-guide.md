## Getting Started with Polymarket

Polymarket is a prediction market platform where you can trade on the outcomes of real-world events. This guide walks you through everything you need to know to use the platform effectively.

## Creating Your Account

Setting up your account:

**Visit Polymarket:** Go to polymarket.com.

**Connect wallet:** Connect a crypto wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.).

**Verify if needed:** Complete verification for full access.

**Fund account:** Deposit USDC to start trading.

**Explore:** Browse markets before trading.

## Navigating the Interface

Understanding the layout:

**Home page:** Featured and popular markets.

**Categories:** Browse markets by category (Politics, Sports, Crypto, etc.).

**Search:** Find specific markets or topics.

**Portfolio:** View your positions and history.

**Account:** Manage settings and funds.

## Finding Markets

How to discover trading opportunities:

**Browse categories:** Explore different market types.

**Search function:** Search for specific topics or keywords.

**Trending markets:** See what's popular.

**New markets:** Find recently created markets.

**Filters:** Filter by volume, end date, or other criteria.

## Understanding Market Pages

What you see on each market:

**Question:** The specific question being asked.

**Current price:** The market's probability assessment.

**Price chart:** Historical price movement.

**Order book:** Current buy and sell orders.

**Resolution criteria:** How the market will be resolved.

**Volume:** Trading activity in the market.

## Reading Prices

Interpreting market prices:

**Price = probability:** $0.65 means 65% implied probability.

**Yes vs. No:** You can buy Yes or No shares.

**Inverse relationship:** Yes at $0.65 means No at $0.35.

**Price movement:** Prices change as opinions change.

**Your assessment:** Compare market price to your view.

## Placing Your First Trade

Step-by-step trading:

**Step 1:** Select a market you want to trade.

**Step 2:** Decide if you think Yes or No.

**Step 3:** Choose order type (Market or Limit).

**Step 4:** Enter the amount you want to trade.

**Step 5:** Review the order details.

**Step 6:** Confirm the transaction.

**Step 7:** Wait for execution.

## Order Types

Different ways to trade:

**Market order:** Buy or sell immediately at current price.

**Limit order:** Set your desired price and wait.

**When to use market:** Need immediate execution.

**When to use limit:** Want better price, can wait.

**Order management:** Can cancel or modify limit orders.

## Managing Your Portfolio

Tracking your positions:

**Portfolio view:** See all your current positions.

**Profit/loss:** Track unrealized and realized gains.

**Position details:** Size, entry price, current value.

**History:** View past trades and outcomes.

**Export:** Download trade history if needed.

## Depositing Funds

Adding money to trade:

**Navigate to deposit:** Find deposit option in account.

**Choose method:** Card, bank transfer, or crypto.

**Enter amount:** Specify how much to deposit.

**Complete transaction:** Follow prompts to finish.

**Wait for funds:** Usually arrives quickly.

## Withdrawing Funds

Taking money out:

**Navigate to withdraw:** Find withdrawal option.

**Enter amount:** Specify how much to withdraw.

**Confirm address:** Verify wallet address.

**Process withdrawal:** Approve the transaction.

**Receive funds:** Usually arrives in minutes.

## Understanding Resolution

How markets resolve:

**Resolution criteria:** Read before trading.

**Resolution date:** When the market will be resolved.

**Resolution source:** What determines the outcome.

**Payout:** Winning shares pay $1, losing shares pay $0.

**Disputes:** Process if resolution is contested.
